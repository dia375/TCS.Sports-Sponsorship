A thought worth exploring

Food for thought · The tech behind the race

Community events that show how TCS tech powers sport.

People see the TCS name on race day. Fewer see the technology behind it. Small events built on what TCS already sponsors could show both, and bring clients along.

Low costUses rights, Run Club and tickets TCS already has. No new venues or big production.
Client valueClients and prospects join as guests, giving sales a warm reason to follow up.
Easy to judgeA small pilot with clear numbers. Only scale what earns its budget.

  • A TCS volunteer gives a 10-minute talk on the tech behind race day.
